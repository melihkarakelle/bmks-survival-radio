**I’d prioritize finding and retaining useful traffic over adding transmit features.** Read the full README and `survival.c`, plus the scan and BK4819 driver paths. Costs below are estimated incremental flash, including compact UI text—not measured build deltas.

| Rank | New feature / emergency benefit | Flash | Implementation and main risk |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **1** | **Priority watch + instant recall.** Check a chosen team/calling channel while searching elsewhere; one shortcut returns immediately. | **0.6–1.0 KB** | Insert a full receive dwell every ~1–2 s using existing 10 ms timers and radio setup (`0x38/0x39` frequency). Preserve scan position and modulation; suspend checks during conversation. **Risk:** one BK4819 means interrupted reception and missed short calls; never describe this as simultaneous watch. |
| **2** | **Mixed-band survival shortlist.** Scan 8–16 personally relevant channels across groups instead of cycling whole bands. | **0.6–1.0 KB** | Store frequency, modulation, bandwidth and optional channel reference; reuse tuning and squelch setup. Persist only explicit edits in audited spare channel storage. **Risk:** stale local channel choices; use explicit RX-only entries and never inherit TX permission from group membership. |
| **3** | **Temporary nuisance skip.** Bypass a continuous carrier, noisy channel or unwanted weather broadcast with one action. | **0.25–0.45 KB** | Reuse `gMR_ChannelExclude`; add a small RAM frequency list for AIR/CUSTOM. Clear on command/reboot, display skip count, handle “all excluded” without falling back to PMR channel 1. **Risk:** skipping real traffic; user-directed only, not automatic permanent blacklisting. |
| **4** | **Recent activity recall.** Recover a frequency after a brief transmission or accidental key press. | **0.4–0.7 KB** | Eight-entry RAM ring: frequency, mode, peak RSSI (`0x67`), uptime age. Log confirmed receive events, deduplicate, recall through normal setup. **Risk:** noise can enter the list; age is elapsed time, not a real timestamp. No audio recording or per-hit EEPROM writes. |
| **5** | **Weak-signal search mode.** Improve the chance of hearing a distant caller that fast scanning misses. | **0.35–0.65 KB** | Add an explicit sensitivity mode: periodic full-dwell sweeps, conservative floor learning, reset floor across bands. FM noise/glitch indicators (`0x65`/`0x63`) can qualify hits; validate AM separately. **Risk:** slower sweeps and more false stops. Current first-sample floor initialization can learn an occupied channel as “noise.” |
| **6** | **Endurance watch preset.** Stretch battery life while waiting on an agreed channel. | **0.3–0.6 KB** | Coordinate existing `BatSav`, backlight, BK1080 shutdown and timed BK4819 sleep/wake; retain PTT responsiveness and low-voltage indication. **Risk:** duty cycling loses short calls/start syllables. Offer continuous watch explicitly; measure current before promising runtime. |
| **7** | **NOAA tone alert watch.** Wake the speaker/display for severe-weather alerts while otherwise quiet or listening to FM broadcast. | **0.5–0.9 KB** | Park BK4819 on a selected WX station. Existing `BK4819_SetCTCSSFrequency(2625)` enables the 1050 Hz detector through `0x51`/`0x07`; qualify detection events with a timer, then latch audio on. **Risk:** regional coverage, false/missed alerts, no geographic filtering; incompatible with continuous scanning on the same receiver. [NWS limitations](https://www.weather.gov/marine/wxradio). |
| **8** | **Optical SOS + call prompt.** Signal nearby rescuers and help a stressed user describe location, incident and assistance needed. | **0.3–0.6 KB** | Nonblocking Morse timing on flashlight GPIO PC3; short LCD prompt pages using existing fonts. Explicit start/cancel, no automatic RF transmission. **Risk:** limited visibility and battery consumption; no delivery acknowledgement or automatic location. |

**Budget:** all eight total roughly **3.3–5.9 KB**, before integration overhead. At 5 KB, implement **1–5 first**, measure the linked binary, then choose the remaining features. Keep transient state in RAM; allocate EEPROM only after auditing the map. The existing `0x1FE8–0x1FFF` area is already assigned—do not assume adjacent calibration bytes are spare.

**Fix before shipping:** `SURVIVAL_TxAllowed()` permits broad intervals, including marine coast frequencies and channel 70; `TX_BLOCKED` is checked only under `TxAll`. Apply immutable exclusions first, validate the **actual offset TX frequency**, and use channel-specific permissions. Firmware permission is not legal authorization. PMR446 at 0.5 W does not make this detachable-antenna radio compliant; FRS also requires an integral antenna. [Ofcom requirements](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/spectrum/business-radio-licences/business-radio-guidance-document.pdf?v=408873), [FCC rules](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title47-vol5/pdf/CFR-2025-title47-vol5-part95.pdf).

**Ideas to reject or defer:**

- **Aviation/broadcast TX, automatic distress transmissions, fake DSC/406 MHz beacons:** interference and false-alert hazards; BK4819 lacks AM TX. An “emergency” menu cannot establish worldwide legal permission.
- **Worldwide TX unlock / useful CB power by firmware:** tuning range is not PA/filter capability. Out-of-band output needs RF measurements and potentially hardware changes.
- **GPS location, maps or rescue acknowledgement:** no GNSS hardware or rescue-network link.
- **Voice recorder, digital-voice decoder, general SDR:** unsuitable for this flash/RAM budget and available signal paths.
- **Guaranteed multi-channel alert monitoring:** only one narrowband receiver. BK1080 adds broadcast reception, not another marine/air/ham receiver.
