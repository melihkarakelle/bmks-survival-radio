== SENDING

== RULE OF 3
You can last about
3 min without air
3 h in cold
  without shelter
3 days no water
3 weeks no food
Order: safety,
  shelter, water,
  signal, food

== WATER
Boil 1 min (3 min
  above 2000 m)
Or filter + tabs
Clear running
  water is better
Need 2-3 L a day

== DIRECTION
Sun rises east,
  sets west
Noon sun: south
  (north half of
  the world)
North star: follow
  the 2 end stars
  of Big Dipper
South half: long
  axis of the
  Southern Cross

== FIRST AID
Check danger first
Bleeding: press
  hard, keep on
Not breathing:
  call help, CPR
  30 push, 2 blow
Keep them warm
  and still
Breaks: do not
  move, support
